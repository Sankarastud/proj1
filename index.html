What is a Profit & Loss Statement?

A Profit & Loss statement, also called an income statement, summarizes revenue and expenses for a specific accounting period and shows whether the business made a profit or loss.

Basic Formula

Gross Profit = Revenue − COGS
Net Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses
